I am an AI researcher specializing in optimal transport, domain adaptation, transfer learning, and foundation models. I completed my PhD in Mathematical Computer Science at Université Paris-Saclay in 2024, where I focused on multi-source domain adaptation through Wasserstein barycenters. Prior to that, I earned a Master's degree in Electronic Engineering and Industrial Informatics from INSA de Rennes and a Bachelor's in Computer Engineering from Universidade Federal do Ceará in Brazil. You can take a look on my recent publications at [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=elSROdcAAAAJ).

Since August 2025 I work as an AI researcher at [Sigma Nova](https://sigmanova.ai), where I work on pre-training, fine-tuning, and transfer learning of large-scale foundation models for neuroscience and time series forecasting. My research bridges theoretical advances in optimal transport with practical machine learning applications, particularly in scenarios where models need to adapt across different domains or data distributions.
